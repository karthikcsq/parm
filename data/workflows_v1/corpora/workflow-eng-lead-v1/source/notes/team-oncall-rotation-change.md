Going from four people to five. Ben joins after two weeks of shadowing.

New rotation order: me, Sam, Mei, Dan, Ben. One week each, handover Monday
morning with a written note in email rather than a verbal one.

Changes I am making at the same time:

  and it is the reason the last three handovers were painless.

  work that week. This was already true in practice and pretending otherwise
  made people feel behind.

  severity. On-call should not be deciding those alone at 2am.

recur under a five-person rotation, but I will check the actual distribution in
August rather than assuming.
