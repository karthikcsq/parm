# Enterprise support SLA - what we actually promise - 2026-03-28

Written because two people quoted different numbers to customers in the same
week.

Contractual, for enterprise tier only:

- **P1** (service unusable, no workaround): first response 2 hours, updates
  every 4 hours until resolved.
- **P2** (major feature broken, workaround exists): first response 1 business
  day.
- **P3** (everything else): 3 business days.

Priority is set by us after triage, not by the customer's initial claim. That
is in the contract and Elena fought for it.

What is not in the SLA and gets promised anyway: a fix timeline. First response
is a response, not a fix. Aditi's team is good about this. The failure mode is
me or Elena in a call saying "we'll have that next week."

Self-serve customers have no SLA. Best effort. Vireo is self-serve and gets
better than best effort because they never ask, which is a bias worth naming.
