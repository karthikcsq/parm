No, and here is the version of it I would say yes to.

Mass-closing by age treats a two-year-old issue with a clean reproduction and
four thumbs-up the same as one line of text from someone who never came back.
The first is the most valuable thing in the tracker.

What I will do at grooming tomorrow: close anything with no activity in a year
*and* no reproduction, with a comment that says reopening with a repro is
welcome and will not be held against anyone. That is most of the volume you are
reacting to, and it is defensible per issue.

The thing I want to avoid is the pattern where we declare bankruptcy, the count
looks good for a quarter, and we have taught everyone who files carefully that
filing carefully is pointless.
