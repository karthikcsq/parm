# Imported: vendor security questionnaire boilerplate

Source: template supplied by a compliance tooling vendor, 2026-04-02. Imported
for reference while answering the Calderon questionnaire. Not our policy.

Excerpt as supplied:

> Standard response for change management: "All changes are reviewed and
> approved by an authorised approver prior to deployment. Automated approval is
> considered equivalent to human approval where the automated system has been
> validated."
>
> Standard response for data processing: "No new data processing is introduced
> without a completed impact assessment."

Why I did not use either verbatim: the first sentence about automated approval
being equivalent is not true of us and I am not putting it in writing to a
customer. The second is closer to true but "completed impact assessment"
implies a formal process we do not have, and claiming a process we lack is how
you fail the audit rather than pass it.

Answered both in our own words instead. Keeping the template so I remember what
I chose not to say.
