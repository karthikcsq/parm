# Meetings we have and why - 2026-02-03

Reviewed because the calendar had grown without anyone deciding it should.

Kept:

- **Monday planning, 30 minutes.** What is in flight, what is blocked. Not
  status theatre; if nothing is blocked it ends in ten minutes and that is a
  good outcome.
- **1:1s, weekly, 30 minutes.** Non-negotiable, and I do not cancel them. A
  cancelled 1:1 is the cheapest thing on my calendar to cancel and the most
  expensive to have cancelled.
- **Design review, on demand.** Called by whoever has a design, not scheduled.

Killed:

- Daily standup. With five people in two timezones it was a status broadcast
  that the Monday meeting already covered.
- The weekly all-team demo. Nobody demoed; it became a second status meeting.

Rule I am trying to hold: a recurring meeting needs someone willing to say what
it is for, in one sentence, without using the word "sync".
