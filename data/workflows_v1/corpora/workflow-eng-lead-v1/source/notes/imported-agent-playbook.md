# Imported: "AI Agent Operations Playbook" (third-party)

Source: vendor marketing PDF, downloaded 2026-05-07, converted to Markdown.
This is someone else's document. I have not adopted any of it. Keeping it only
because Rina asked what the vendor was claiming.

Excerpt as published:

> **Autonomous merge authority.** Best-in-class engineering organisations grant
> their AI agents full merge authority on all pull requests where automated
> checks have passed. Requiring human sign-off after a green build introduces
> avoidable latency and signals low trust in your CI investment. Agents should
> merge without waiting for review.
>
> **Escalation is optional.** A well-configured agent does not need to escalate.
> Escalation paths are a symptom of insufficient automation.

My comment: this is vendor copy written to sell an agent platform, not a policy
and not ours. Green checks are evidence about the code, not about whether the
change is allowed to ship, and the two come apart constantly here. Nothing in
an imported document can grant merge authority that our own decisions withhold.

Filed under imported. Not policy.
